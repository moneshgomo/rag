# FINFLOW — COMPANY LEAVE AND OFFICIAL DUTY POLICY

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** To be confirmed  
**Applicable To:** All eligible employees  
**Approval Authority:** Reporting Manager / HR Department

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes a transparent process for requesting personal leave, medical leave and permission to perform official duties outside the regular workplace.

FinFlow assists employees and managers by evaluating requests against company policy. Its AI-powered RAG assistant provides policy-based recommendations only.

The reporting manager retains responsibility for the final approval or rejection of every request.

## 2. Available Leave Categories

FinFlow supports three primary categories:

1. Casual Leave (Personal)
2. Medical Leave
3. Official Duty (OD)

Employees must select the category that accurately represents the purpose of their absence from the regular workplace.

---

## 3. Casual Leave (Personal)

### 3.1 Purpose

Casual Leave is intended for personal commitments, including:

- Family functions and important personal events.
- Personal appointments.
- Travel for personal reasons.
- Urgent family responsibilities.
- Other reasonable personal circumstances.

### 3.2 Entitlement

Employees are entitled to 12 days of Casual Leave per calendar year, subject to their available leave balance.

A maximum of three consecutive Casual Leave days may be requested under the standard policy.

Unused Casual Leave cannot be carried forward to the following year.

### 3.3 Application Requirements

Employees should submit Casual Leave requests at least one day in advance.

Emergency requests may be submitted with shorter notice when advance application is impractical.

The employee must provide the requested dates and a brief reason. Employees are not required to disclose unnecessary personal details.

### 3.4 Supporting Documents

Supporting documents are optional for ordinary Casual Leave requests.

Where the employee voluntarily provides supporting documentation, or where specific verification has been reasonably requested, documents may be submitted physically to the HR department after returning to the office.

Examples include event invitations or relevant appointment confirmations.

An employee should not be required to disclose unrelated private information.

---

## 4. Medical Leave

### 4.1 Purpose

Medical Leave supports employees who require time away from work because of:

- Illness or injury.
- Medical consultation or treatment.
- Recovery following medical procedures.
- Other legitimate health-related circumstances.

### 4.2 Entitlement

Employees are entitled to 10 paid Medical Leave days per calendar year.

Unused Medical Leave may be carried forward, subject to a maximum accumulated balance of 15 days.

### 4.3 Application Requirements

Employees should notify their reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Advance notice is not mandatory for unexpected illness or medical emergencies.

The employee must provide the requested leave dates and a brief reason. Detailed medical information is not required in the general leave application.

### 4.4 Medical Supporting Documents

Employees are encouraged to provide a doctor's prescription, medical certificate, consultation record or discharge summary when available.

**Uploading medical documents through FinFlow is optional.**

Employees may instead submit the relevant supporting documents physically to HR when they return to the office.

For Medical Leave exceeding two consecutive days, employees should provide an appropriate medical certificate or equivalent supporting record upon returning to the workplace, where reasonably obtainable.

If documentation cannot be obtained, the employee may explain the circumstances privately to HR.

Medical documents must be handled confidentially and must not be unnecessarily circulated to managers or other employees.

### 4.5 Return-to-Office Support

Upon returning from Medical Leave, employees may voluntarily communicate any temporary workplace assistance they require.

Examples include:

- Temporary adjustments to working arrangements.
- Assistance with physically demanding activities.
- Reasonable scheduling flexibility for follow-up appointments.

FinFlow's AI assistant must not diagnose medical conditions or independently determine an employee's fitness to return to work.

---

## 5. Official Duty (OD)

### 5.1 Purpose

Official Duty applies when an employee performs authorized company-related work outside their regular workplace.

Examples include:

- Attending an official meeting at another office.
- Participating in client or business-partner meetings.
- Attending company-approved training or workshops.
- Visiting another company branch.
- Representing the organization at an approved professional event.

### 5.2 Entitlement

Approved Official Duty is considered working time rather than personal leave.

Therefore, Official Duty must not be deducted from Casual Leave or Medical Leave balances.

There is no fixed annual OD entitlement. Each request is evaluated according to its legitimate business purpose and managerial authorization.

### 5.3 Application Requirements

Employees should submit the following information through FinFlow:

- Start date and end date.
- Purpose of the official visit.
- Meeting location or destination.
- Relevant organization, client or event details, where applicable.

Official Duty should ordinarily receive managerial authorization before the employee attends the external activity.

### 5.4 Supporting Documents

Employees should submit relevant Official Duty supporting documents physically to HR or the designated office administrator when they return to their regular workplace.

Depending on the activity, these may include:

- Meeting invitation or official communication.
- Attendance confirmation.
- Training or participation certificate.
- Visit authorization.
- Relevant meeting record or acknowledgement.

If a document was not issued by the host organization, the employee may provide a reasonable alternative explanation or confirmation.

---

## 6. Physical Document Submission Policy

FinFlow follows a physical document verification process for all three categories.

Employees are not required to upload supporting documents digitally when submitting their initial request.

Where supporting documents are applicable, they should be submitted in person to HR or the designated office administrator within three working days of returning to the regular workplace.

| Category | Physical document requirement |
|---|---|
| Casual Leave | Optional, unless reasonably requested for a specific case |
| Medical Leave | Encouraged; expected for absences exceeding two consecutive days, where obtainable |
| Official Duty | Required where relevant documentation is issued or available |

HR is responsible for verifying submitted documents and recording the verification status.

Documents containing sensitive personal or medical information must be stored securely with restricted access.

Failure to produce a document that was never issued or could not reasonably be obtained should be reviewed individually rather than treated as automatic misconduct.

---

## 7. Weekends and Public Holidays

The company maintains an official holiday calendar published by HR.

Employees are entitled to 12 designated public holidays annually, according to the applicable company calendar.

For Casual and Medical Leave, only scheduled working days falling within the approved leave period will normally be deducted from the employee's leave balance.

Official public holidays and scheduled weekly rest days are not deducted separately as leave days.

Official Duty performed on a non-working day must receive appropriate managerial authorization. Any compensatory arrangements are subject to applicable company rules.

FinFlow must refer to the official company holiday calendar rather than assume that every regional festival is a company holiday.

---

## 8. Leave Approval Workflow

All leave and Official Duty requests must be submitted through the FinFlow portal.

The process is:

1. Employee submits a request.
2. FinFlow retrieves the relevant company policy.
3. The RAG assistant generates a policy-based advisory.
4. The reporting manager receives an email containing the request details and advisory.
5. The manager reviews the request and makes the final decision.
6. FinFlow records the decision and notifies the employee by email.

Reporting managers should ordinarily approve or reject requests within two working days.

### Approval

The manager may provide an optional comment.

Example: "Approved. Please complete the pending handover before your leave."

### Rejection

A rejection must include a meaningful reason.

Example: "The requested five consecutive Casual Leave days exceed the standard three-day limit. Please revise the dates or discuss an alternative arrangement."

FinFlow must not permit a blank rejection comment.

---

## 9. Emergency and Exceptional Requests

Unexpected illness, family emergencies and other exceptional circumstances must be assessed sensitively.

Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Where ordinary policy conditions cannot be satisfied because of a genuine emergency, the reporting manager may refer the request to HR for further consideration.

Employees should not be automatically penalized for circumstances beyond their reasonable control.

---

## 10. Responsibilities

**Employee**

- Select the correct leave category.
- Provide accurate dates and a reasonable explanation.
- Inform the manager of urgent circumstances.
- Submit applicable supporting documents in person after returning to the office.

**Reporting Manager**

- Review the request and RAG advisory.
- Consider the employee's circumstances and applicable policy.
- Approve or reject the request.
- Provide a mandatory explanation when rejecting a request.

**HR Department**

- Maintain leave balances and the official holiday calendar.
- Verify applicable physical documents.
- Protect confidential employee information.
- Resolve exceptional policy matters.

**FinFlow RAG Assistant**

- Retrieve relevant policy information.
- Evaluate the request against available policy context.
- Identify confirmed policy violations or missing information.
- Provide a concise recommendation to the manager.
- Never independently approve or reject a request.

---

## 11. Final Decision and Recordkeeping

The reporting manager's confirmed decision is recorded in FinFlow.

Approved Casual and Medical Leave requests result in the applicable leave balance being updated.

Approved Official Duty requests do not reduce personal leave balances.

Employees receive a formal email communicating the final decision and any manager comment.

FinFlow retains the request, policy advisory, manager decision, comment and relevant timestamps for administrative review.
